Purpose

Why this framework exists

Every Account Officer and Account Manager must be able to run the full sales presentation and qualify a clinic without medical support in most cases. The Medical Team is a conditional resource, engaged only when a clinic's clinical questions genuinely exceed the account owner's knowledge — never a default step in the funnel.

This guide covers all 11 phases, from prospecting to the full Clinic Success handover at month 6. Phases 1–10 run inside the standard 45-day sales cycle. Account Officers and Account Managers follow the same funnel; Account Managers also lead their territory team and carry its combined target.
